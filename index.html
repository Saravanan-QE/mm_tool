What is a SIP and how does it work?
A Systematic Investment Plan lets you invest a fixed amount in mutual funds monthly, harnessing compounding. Our SIP Calculator estimates future returns based on contribution, expected rate, and tenure.

Our FD Calculator estimates maturity and interest earned. Input principal, tenure, and compounding frequency using current rates from SBI, HDFC, and other banks.
