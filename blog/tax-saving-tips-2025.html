Top Tax-Saving Tips for FY 2025-26

Navigating India's tax landscape for FY 2025-26 requires strategic planning to minimize your tax liability while maximizing savings. With evolving tax regimes and updated slabs, understanding your options is crucial for salaried professionals, freelancers, and business owners. The Indian Income Tax Act offers numerous deductions and exemptions under sections like 80C, 80D, and HRA, enabling you to save significantly when planned wisely.

Understanding Tax Regimes

For FY 2025-26, Indian taxpayers can choose between the Old Tax Regime, which offers deductions, and the New Tax Regime, with lower tax rates but fewer exemptions. The New Regime has an increased standard deduction (₹75,000 for salaried individuals) and simplified tax slabs, but it eliminates benefits like Section 80C or HRA exemptions. Use our Income Tax Calculator to compare both regimes.

Key Deductions to Explore

  • Section 80C (Up to ₹1.5 Lakh): PPF, ELSS, NSC, 5-year tax-saving FDs.
  • Section 80D (₹25K–₹50K): Health insurance premiums (higher cap for seniors).
  • HRA Exemption: Live in rented accommodation — use the HRA Calculator for the exact exempt amount.
  • Section 80TTA/80TTB: Savings account interest (₹10K) or senior deposits (₹50K).

Tax-Saving Investment Strategies

  • ELSS Funds: Tax benefit under 80C plus 10-12% potential returns.
  • NPS: Extra ₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B). Try the NPS Calculator.
  • Home Loan Interest: Up to ₹2L deduction under Section 24(b).

By leveraging these strategies, a salaried individual earning ₹10 lakh could save over ₹1 lakh by fully utilizing Section 80C, 80D, and HRA in the Old Regime. Use our Income Tax Calculator to simulate scenarios.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Section 80C deduction limit?
₹1.5 lakh, covering instruments like PPF, ELSS, NSC, and 5-year tax-saving FDs — available only under the Old Regime.
What is the Section 80D limit for health insurance?
₹25,000 generally, with a higher cap for senior citizen premiums, up to ₹50,000.
Does the New Regime allow HRA or 80C deductions?
No — the New Regime offers a higher standard deduction (₹75,000 for salaried individuals) and simplified slabs but eliminates 80C, HRA and similar exemptions.
How much can a ₹10 lakh earner save using all deductions?
Over ₹1 lakh, by fully utilizing Section 80C, Section 80D, and HRA exemption together under the Old Regime.
What additional deduction does NPS offer?
An extra ₹50,000 under Section 80CCD(1B), over and above the ₹1.5 lakh Section 80C limit.
