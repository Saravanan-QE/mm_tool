Income Tax Slabs FY 2025-26: Zero Tax Up to ₹12.75 Lakh – Budget 2025 Highlights

The Union Budget 2025 (presented February 1, 2025) delivered major relief for middle-class taxpayers, making incomes up to ₹12 lakh tax-free under the new regime (or ₹12.75 lakh for salaried with ₹75,000 standard deduction). This benefits ~85% of filers, per experts. Old regime slabs remain unchanged, but the new regime's revised structure (with a 25% slab up to ₹24 lakh) favors those with fewer deductions. Use our updated Income Tax Calculator to compare regimes and estimate savings.

New Regime Tax Slabs (Post-Budget 2025)

Applies to income above ₹12 lakh (after rebate u/s 87A):

  • ₹0 – ₹4,00,000: 0% (exemption + rebate)
  • ₹4,00,001 – ₹8,00,000: 5%
  • ₹8,00,001 – ₹12,00,000: 10%
  • ₹12,00,001 – ₹16,00,000: 15%
  • ₹16,00,001 – ₹20,00,000: 20%
  • ₹20,00,001 – ₹24,00,000: 25% (new slab)
  • Above ₹24,00,000: 30%

Key Changes & Who Benefits

Budget 2025 hiked the standard deduction to ₹75,000 (from ₹50,000) and extended updated ITR filing to 4 years. Seniors get TDS relief on interest (up to ₹1 lakh, doubled from ₹50,000).

  • Middle-Class Salaried (₹10–15L income): Save up to ₹17,500 annually in new regime.
  • High-Deduction Filers: Stick to old regime for 80C (₹1.5L), 80D (₹25K–₹50K), home loan interest (₹2L).
  • NPS Contributors: Extra ₹50,000 deduction u/s 80CCD(1B) extended to NPS Vatsalya.

Example: ₹15 Lakh Salaried Income

New Regime: After ₹75,000 standard deduction (taxable ₹14.25L), tax works out to ₹97,500. Old Regime with no deductions beyond the ₹50,000 standard deduction (taxable ₹14.5L): ₹2,57,400. Savings: ₹1,59,900.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the new zero-tax income threshold?
₹12 lakh under the new regime, or ₹12.75 lakh for salaried taxpayers after the ₹75,000 standard deduction, following Budget 2025.
What are the new regime tax slabs after Budget 2025?
0% up to ₹4 lakh, 5% from ₹4-8 lakh, 10% from ₹8-12 lakh, 15% from ₹12-16 lakh, 20% from ₹16-20 lakh, a new 25% slab from ₹20-24 lakh, and 30% above ₹24 lakh.
How much can a ₹15 lakh salaried income save under the new regime?
Tax works out to ₹97,500 under the New Regime after the ₹75,000 standard deduction (taxable ₹14.25L), versus ₹2,57,400 under the Old Regime with no other deductions (taxable ₹14.5L after its ₹50,000 standard deduction) — a saving of ₹1,59,900.
Did anything change for senior citizens?
Yes — TDS relief on interest income was doubled, from ₹50,000 to ₹1 lakh.
Who should still consider the Old Regime?
Taxpayers with large deductions — Section 80C (₹1.5 lakh), Section 80D (₹25,000-50,000), and home loan interest (₹2 lakh) — may save more under the Old Regime despite its unchanged slabs.
