Retirement Planning Strategies for FY 2025-26

Retirement planning is crucial for financial independence in India's evolving economy. With rising life expectancy and 4-5% inflation, a corpus of ₹5-10 crore may be needed (assuming ₹10L annual expenses and 30 years of retirement). Our Retirement Planning Calculator helps estimate your target.

Key Retirement Investment Options

  • NPS: 8-10% returns; tax benefits under 80C + extra ₹50K under 80CCD(1B). Try our NPS Calculator.
  • PPF: 7-7.5% tax-free, 15-year lock-in, 80C eligible.
  • Equity MF SIPs: 10-12% returns long-term. ₹10K SIP at 12% → ₹1 crore in 25 years.
  • Fixed Deposits: Short-term safety at 6.5-7.5%.

Building Your Plan

  • Estimate Expenses: Project today's monthly expense and inflate at 5%/yr.
  • Set Corpus Goal: 25-30x annual expenses. ₹10L/yr → ₹2.5-3 crore.
  • Diversify: 60% equity, 30% debt, 10% liquid.
  • Review Annually: Bump NPS/SIP 10%/year.

Frequently Asked Questions

How large a retirement corpus might I need?
Roughly ₹5-10 crore, assuming ₹10 lakh in annual expenses today, 4-5% inflation, and a 30-year retirement.
What returns can different retirement investments offer?
NPS: 8-10%; PPF: 7-7.5% tax-free; equity mutual fund SIPs: 10-12% long-term; fixed deposits: 6.5-7.5% for short-term safety.
What corpus multiple of annual expenses is recommended?
A common guideline is 25-30 times your annual expenses — for ₹10 lakh/year in expenses, that's roughly ₹2.5-3 crore.
What asset allocation is suggested for a retirement portfolio?
Around 60% equity, 30% debt, and 10% liquid funds, reviewed and rebalanced annually.
Should retirement contributions increase over time?
Yes — bumping NPS or SIP contributions by about 10% each year helps the corpus keep pace with rising expenses and inflation.
