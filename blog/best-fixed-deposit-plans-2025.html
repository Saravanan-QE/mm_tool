Plan Your Fixed Deposits Wisely: Using Our FD Calculator for FY 2025-26

Fixed Deposits (FDs) are a cornerstone of safe investing in India, offering guaranteed returns and capital protection. For FY 2025-26, interest rates range from 5.5% to 7.5% across banks like SBI, HDFC, and ICICI. Seniors typically get an additional 0.5%. Use our Fixed Deposit Calculator to estimate maturity accurately.

Factors to Consider

  • Interest Rates: SBI 6.5–7%; HDFC/ICICI up to 7.5%; Post Office 6.9–7.5%.
  • Tenure: Short (1-2 yrs) for liquidity; 5-yr tax-saving FDs qualify for 80C.
  • Compounding: Quarterly compounding edges out yearly. ₹5L at 7% for 5 yrs → ₹5.38L (Q) vs ₹5.35L (Y).
  • Senior Citizen Benefits: Extra 0.25-0.5%.
  • Premature Withdrawal: 0.5-1% penalty — check before locking.

Top FD Plans for FY 2025-26

  • SBI: 6.5-7% for 1-5 yrs; Amrit Vrishti 400-day at 7.25%.
  • HDFC Bank: 6.7-7.5% for 1-10 yrs.
  • Post Office: 6.9-7.5% for 1-5 yrs, government-backed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What FD interest rates are available in FY 2025-26?
Rates run roughly 5.5% to 7.5% across major banks. SBI offers about 6.5-7%, HDFC and ICICI go up to 7.5%, and Post Office FDs run 6.9-7.5%.
Do senior citizens get a higher FD rate?
Yes, senior citizens typically get an additional 0.25-0.5% over the standard rate on the same tenure.
Does compounding frequency change FD returns?
Yes. On ₹5 lakh at 7% for 5 years, quarterly compounding yields about ₹5.38 lakh versus ₹5.35 lakh for yearly compounding.
What happens if I withdraw an FD before maturity?
Premature withdrawal usually attracts a penalty of 0.5-1%, reducing the effective interest earned — check the terms before locking in a long tenure.
What is FD laddering?
Splitting a lump sum across multiple tenures — for example, dividing ₹15 lakh into 1-year, 3-year and 5-year FDs — so you get liquidity at regular intervals while still earning longer-term rates on part of the money.
