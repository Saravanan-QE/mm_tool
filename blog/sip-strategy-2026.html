Best SIP Strategy for 2026: Should You Increase Contributions?

Published 22 May 2026. With CPI at 6.35% (May 2026 print, above RBI's 4% target midpoint) and Nifty 50 crossing 27,800 in June 2026, flat SIPs are losing ground in real terms. AMFI's April 2026 data shows industry SIP inflows at ₹31,400 crore — a record month, with step-up SIPs driving 42% of that. If you started at ₹5,000 in 2020, staying at ₹5,000 today has silently cut your real contribution by ~30%. Model the fix with our Step-Up SIP Calculator.

Why Step-Up Beats Flat SIPs in 2026

  • Inflation neutralisation: A 10% annual step-up beats 6.35% CPI comfortably — your real investing capacity grows.
  • Compounding leverage: ₹10,000/month at 12% flat over 25 years → ₹1.9 crore. Same start with 10% annual step-up → ₹4.9 crore.
  • Salary sync: If your CTC rises 8-10% yearly, aligning SIP increments removes the "annual review, forget to update SIP" gap.
  • SEBI-mandated risk-o-meter refresh: The March 2026 update makes it easier to spot funds whose risk profile drifted upward — rebalance at review.

Market Backdrop

Sensex touched 91,200 in early June 2026. Historical 5-year rolling equity returns for Nifty 50: 13.8% (as of March 2026, per AMFI). Debt fund category average: 6.9%. Balanced advantage funds: 10.4%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a flat SIP lose to inflation over time?
With CPI at 6.35% in May 2026, a SIP amount left unchanged since 2020 has effectively lost about 30% of its real contribution value.
How much more can a step-up SIP earn versus a flat SIP?
₹10,000/month at 12% flat over 25 years grows to about ₹1.9 crore; the same SIP with a 10% annual step-up grows to about ₹4.9 crore.
What SIP inflow trend did AMFI report for April 2026?
A record ₹31,400 crore in industry SIP inflows, with step-up SIPs accounting for 42% of that.
What are recent 5-year rolling equity returns?
Nifty 50's 5-year rolling average was 13.8% as of March 2026, versus 6.9% for the debt fund category average and 10.4% for balanced advantage funds.
When should I schedule my SIP step-up?
Ideally in April, right after your annual salary appraisal, rather than January — many AMCs now auto-schedule this via the Bharat SIP mandate.
